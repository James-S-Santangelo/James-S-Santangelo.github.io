The Santangelo Lab is recruiting undergraduate and graduate students. We use
field experiments, genomics, and computational approaches to study how plants
adapt to rapid environmental change. You can read more on the
[Research](/research/) and [Publications](/publications/) pages. 

We welcome applicants from all backgrounds. You don't need experience in every
method we use. Curiosity, persistence, and a willingness to learn matter more.

## Undergraduate students

uOttawa Biology students can do a fourth-year honours research project in the lab
([BIO4009 or BIO4004](https://www.uottawa.ca/faculty-science/research-publications/research-opportunities-students)).
There may also be paid summer or part-time positions (e.g., NSERC USRA,
work-study). Email me with your CV, an unofficial transcript, and a few sentences
on what interests you about the lab's research.

## Graduate students (MSc and PhD)

I'm always looking for keen and motivated graduate students. If any of the
[Research themes](/Research/) interest you, please reach out. Applications are
welcome anytime, though the program requires that **your supervisor be
identified at the time you apply** (see [admission program
requirements](https://www.uottawa.ca/study/graduate-studies/program-specific-requirements). 

**To get in touch**, email me at
[jsantang@uottawa.ca](mailto:jsantang@uottawa.ca) with the subject line
*"Prospective MSc/PhD student – Your Name"* and attach:

1. Your CV
2. Unofficial transcripts
3. A short statement: which project or questions interest you,
   relevant experience (research, field, lab, or coding), and what you hope to get
   out of graduate school.

**What happens next:** if there's a good fit, we'll set up a time to chat
about projects and expectations, and I'll put you in touch with current and former lab
members so you can ask them about the lab. After that, you'll submit a formal
application to uOttawa naming me as your supervisor.

Graduate students at uOttawa are funded through a mix of TAships, RAships, and
scholarships. I encourage all students to apply for external funding through
NSERC
([MSc](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/canada-graduate-research-scholarship-masters-program),
[PhD](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/canada-graduate-research-scholarship-doctoral-program))
and/or
[OGS](https://www.uottawa.ca/study/graduate-studies/funding-financing/awards/ontario),
and I'm happy to help in putting an application together.

## Postdoctoral researchers

There are no funded postdoctoral positions advertised right now, but I'm happy to
support strong candidates applying for independent fellowships (e.g., NSERC
Postdoctoral Fellowships or Banting). Please email me your CV and a short
description of the research you'd like to pursue.
